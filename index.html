Welcome to ComServe

ComServe helps local communities organise events, share notices, and collect community contributions in a simple and connected way.

Use the platform to keep residents informed, support local activities, and take part in community life from one place.

How ComServe Works

Create an account, choose your community, and begin browsing events, notices, and projects that matter to you.

Residents can respond to activities, make contributions where needed, and receive updates as community plans progress.
