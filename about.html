About ComServe

ComServe is a community platform created to help residents organise events, share important notices, and contribute to local activities with greater clarity and coordination.

The Problem We Aim to Solve

Many local communities rely on scattered messages, informal announcements, and disconnected contribution methods. This can make it difficult for residents to stay informed, respond on time, or take part in shared events.

How ComServe Helps

ComServe gives residents a central place to communicate, participate in community events, and contribute to activities that support neighbours, families, and local projects.

It also helps community leaders publish updates, manage participation, and keep records of the activities happening within their area.

Main Goals

  • Improve communication within communities.
  • Make it easier to organise and join events.
  • Support transparent community contributions.
  • Help residents receive timely notices and updates.

How It Works

  1. Create an account.
  2. Join or select your community.
  3. View community events and notices.
  4. RSVP or contribute to events.
  5. Receive updates.
